Who we are

Medina Recruitment Consultant ("Medina", "we") is a Ministry of Manpower–licensed employment agency in Singapore (UEN 53003811X), located at 511 Guillemard Road #03-10, Singapore 399849. This policy explains how we handle personal data when you contact us or use our services, including messages sent to us on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, our websites, and our helper selection portal.

What we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data — your name, phone number, social media handle, and the contents of messages or comments you send to our pages and accounts.
  • Service data — information needed to provide employment agency services, such as household requirements, and for clients engaging our services, documents required by Singapore regulations (e.g. work permit applications).

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To provide and administer our employment agency services.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations under Singapore law, including the Employment Agencies Act and MOM requirements.

Messaging channels

When you message our Facebook Page or Instagram account, or comment on our posts and advertisements, those messages and comments (and your public profile name) are received through Meta's platforms and stored in our customer management system so our staff can respond to you and keep track of your enquiry. Replies are sent by our staff. We do not use your messages for advertising to third parties and we do not sell your personal data.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored in access-controlled cloud systems used only by Medina staff. We share personal data only with service providers that host our systems, with government authorities where required by law (e.g. MOM work pass processing), and with parties you ask us to share it with as part of our services (e.g. an insurer, for a policy you purchase through us).

Retention

We retain enquiry and service records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet Singapore regulatory record-keeping requirements, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

Your rights & data deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data — including messages and comments we hold from Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp — by contacting us using the details below. To request deletion, tell us the channel and the name/handle you contacted us from; we will delete the associated records within 30 days unless we are legally required to retain them, and will confirm once done.
